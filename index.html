Wainwright & Son.Builders. Honiton. Since 1982.

Wainwright & Son is a family-run building firm based in Honiton, Devon. David Wainwright founded the company in 1982, working across East Devon on extensions and renovation projects. His son Tom joined the business in 2015 and now runs day-to-day operations on site, leading a small, reliable team of three to four tradesmen. The business remains wholly family-owned.

We build house extensions, full renovations and refurbishments, and barn and outbuilding conversions — taking each project from groundwork through to finished joinery. Recent work includes a two-storey side extension in Ottery St Mary, a full farmhouse renovation near Honiton, and a detached barn conversion into a dwelling in Payhembury. We do not subcontract design or structural work; we build what the drawings and calculations specify.

Our work is within a 15-mile radius of Honiton, covering Honiton, Ottery St Mary, Sidmouth, Exeter outskirts, and the villages in between. We know the local building styles, materials, and planning context, and that knowledge saves time on every job.
