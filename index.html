Who we are

David Wainwright started the business in 1982, working weekends and evenings while holding down a full-time job. Within three years he was working for himself full-time, taking on extensions and renovations across East Devon.

His son Tom joined in 2015, bringing a City & Guilds qualification in carpentry and a fresh approach to project management. Tom now runs the day-to-day on site, while David oversees planning and client relationships.

We don't subcontract design or structural work — we build what the drawings say, and we do it properly. No job goes off-site until the client's happy.
